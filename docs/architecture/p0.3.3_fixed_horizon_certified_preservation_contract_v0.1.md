# P0.3.3 — Fixed-T Certified Shared-Preservation Contract v0.1

Status: FROZEN / PASS after focused independent audit.

## Purpose and frozen theorem mapping

```matlab
decision = avlc.analysis.evaluateFixedHorizonFeasibility(scenario,z0,T,options);
```

This is a **certified inner fixed-T evaluator**, not a complete exact numerical
solver. It seeks a certificate that one shared progressive prefix preserves
both execution options through the supplied T. It introduces no new theory
component, longitudinal objective or underlying dynamics implementation.

The unique theorem source is `AV_lane_change_Self_Contained_Core_Proof_Master.md`,
SHA-256 `5db01432a1fc63dc966f8aa5dfb5428cb6758da44545db3fe0330896eb4043ab`.
The direct sources are P5b-Lift B1–B4 and Part IX §§136–145, especially P5b-T3.
The exact finite-chart theorem states that, under Lift assumptions, preservation
through T exists iff some admissible rho and true safe moments satisfy

```text
rho in [rhoLower, A*T]
(eta,zeta) in M_x_safe(T)
[yMinus,yPlus] intersect [YTin,Yfwd] is nonempty
```

The current computation uses only one certified longitudinal witness and a
finite rho grid. This is an inner subset of the theorem's existential domain:
success proves feasibility, but search/grid failure proves no infeasibility.
Part-X §175 explicitly separates this finite-Lift representation from the
entire global closed feasible-time set. No scalar maximum is assumed.

## Authoritative status semantics and priority

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| CERTIFIED_FEASIBLE | Zero-duration global membership, or a complete positive-T P5b-T3 certificate |
| INFEASIBILITY_CERTIFIED | Only initial global nonmembership, or defined initial GF2 NOT_PRESERVABLE for T>0 |
| OUTSIDE_PROVED_DOMAIN | Initial membership holds, no GF2 negative was obtained, but the finite-Lift gate fails |
| UNRESOLVED | Numerical search, finite grid or cross-check did not produce a certificate; never a negative theorem claim |

The flags `feasibleCertified` and `infeasibleCertified` are mutually exclusive.
Both are false for OUTSIDE_PROVED_DOMAIN and UNRESOLVED. The two corresponding
certificate fields are empty except in their matching certified status.

Evaluation order is deliberate:

1. Evaluate frozen global closed membership at z0.
2. Nonmembership immediately gives INITIAL_GLOBAL_NONMEMBERSHIP, including T=0.
3. For T=0, membership alone gives ZERO_DURATION_GLOBAL_MEMBERSHIP. No capability,
   GF2, Lift, longitudinal search, linprog or rho grid is called.
4. For T>0, call frozen capability, diagnostics and local GF2 in that order.
   A defined NOT_PRESERVABLE gives INITIAL_LOCAL_INCOMPATIBILITY, before Lift.
5. GF2 UNDEFINED is not incompatibility. Independently evaluate the Lift gate.
6. Only an applicable gate proceeds to one longitudinal search and the rho grid.

Thus exact GF2 negatives are not wrongly restricted to the narrower Lift
domain, and local UNDEFINED cannot exclude a state from global preservation.

## P5b-Lift domain certificate

`domain.p5bLift` is populated only when its positive-T gate is evaluated.
It is an empty struct on paths that do not need Lift, rather than a fabricated
failed domain result for T=0.

```text
finiteTR,finiteTF,finiteTG,finiteHT
deltaF,deltaR,deltaT,strictDiscriminants
cO0,cOPositive,yNonnegative,wNonnegative
controlBoundsFrozen,baseDefinitionFrozen
applicable,reason
```

Reuse cap0's TRsafe, TFsafe, TG and HT; do not copy opportunity root formulas.
On finite clocks compute only the Master's discriminant coordinates:

```text
deltaF = cF0-B*TFsafe
deltaR = cR0-C*TRsafe
deltaT = cT0-C*HT
cO0 = cR0+cF0
```

The gate requires all four clocks finite; all three deltas finite and strictly
positive; cO0>0; y0,w0>=0; and the frozen control-box/base-definition declarations.
`controlBoundsFrozen=true` and `baseDefinitionFrozen=true` describe the reduced
model, not a substitute for path certification. On success, actual longitudinal
box/gap safety comes from the frozen witness certificate, and progressive
lateral box reachability from the exact lateral theorem. These together supply
the remaining path assumptions and Part-VI base admissibility throughout the
prefix; no extra transient constraint is silently added.

Reasons prioritize OUTSIDE_PROGRESSIVE_DOMAIN, NONPOSITIVE_INITIAL_CO,
INFINITE_INITIAL_OPPORTUNITY and NONSTRICT_INITIAL_DISCRIMINANT. Multiple
checks can fail together. Deltas for infinite clocks are NaN because their
finite-clock coordinates are unavailable. In particular, exact stopping-gap
discriminant equality is represented by an infinite frozen opportunity and
fails the finite-clock gate; no square-root formula is copied merely to
manufacture a zero delta diagnostic. Nonpositive cO likewise often coexists
with infinite clocks. Every check remains visible independently of reason.

The gate does not inherit GF2's stricter y>0, w>0, p/q>0 or auxiliary-slack
restrictions. The Lift proof supplies valid finite reserve charts by its own
discriminant/opportunity/p-coordinate arguments. Endpoint contact and wT=0
are not automatically rejected as local-chart degeneracies.

## Longitudinal reuse and options

For applicable positive-T states, call the frozen P0.3.2b searcher **once**.
Retain its entire result under `longitudinalSearch`. A result other than
WITNESS_FOUND gives UNRESOLVED / LONGITUDINAL_SEARCH_UNRESOLVED and no rho
candidates. Its LP diagnostic is never an infeasibility certificate here.

Even WITNESS_FOUND must contain `certificate.validWitness=true` and
`certificate.status=SAFE_WITNESS`; otherwise return
LONGITUDINAL_CERTIFICATE_INCONSISTENT. Consume its terminal state, moments
and witness directly; do not reverify or recompute them in production.

| Option | Default | Ownership |
| --- | --- | --- |
| numSegments | 8 | Forward unchanged to frozen searcher |
| maxCutIterations | 20 | Forward unchanged to frozen searcher |
| numRhoSamples | 33 | This evaluator validates a finite real integer >=2 |

Only these option names and a scalar options struct are accepted. Forwarded
value validation is deliberately delegated to the searcher **when search is
actually consumed**. T=0 and exact/domain early exits do not call that searcher
just to validate unused longitudinal settings. This avoids copying its private
validation/search logic. Own request T and rho-option validation occur up front.

## Finite rho search and geometry probe

Call frozen lateral reachability with rho=A*T to obtain exact allocation
bounds. Build `unique(linspace(rhoLower,rhoUpper,numRhoSamples)')`, using exact
double equality. No optimizer, root finder, randomization or adaptive grid is
used. Evaluate candidates in increasing order and return the first certificate.

For each candidate call frozen lateral reachability. Its defined/admissible
result supplies wT, yMinus, yPlus and yBarT; none of those formulas is copied.
Construct zProbe from the longitudinal terminal coordinates, `w=reach.wT`,
`y=reach.yMinus`, then call frozen P0.2.7 capability.

Only the finite geometry slice is authoritative at this step: YO, YTin, YTout,
Yfwd and finiteGeometryAvailable. The probe's dualCapable Boolean is **not**
used as terminal truth. In a dedicated test, the probe is not dual-capable,
but its slice contains another reachable terminal y that is dual-capable.

If geometry is unavailable or any required frontier is nonfinite, retain the
original frozen sentinel values, leave margins NaN and overlap false, record
FINITE_TERMINAL_GEOMETRY_UNAVAILABLE, and continue. There is no fabricated
finite corridor, NaN-to-Inf replacement or global-membership fallback corridor.

## Exact overlap, margins and actual terminal point

With L=capProbe.completion.YTin and U=capProbe.joint.Yfwd, compute

```text
M_corr = U-L
M_dead = reach.yPlus-L
M_fwd = U-reach.yMinus
minMargin = min(M_corr,M_dead,M_fwd)
overlap = M_corr>=0 && M_dead>=0 && M_fwd>=0
```

No epsilon enters these comparisons. Phi_dual is a diagnostic:

```text
m = L+(U-L)/2
distance = max(reach.yMinus-m, m-reach.yPlus, 0)
phiDual = (U-L)/2-distance
```

This is the Master's midpoint-distance formula with algebraically equivalent
midpoint evaluation. It is not optimized or used to replace the three-margin
truth predicate. The exact Boolean equivalence is tested on dyadic contact
fixtures and deterministic candidate grids; ordinary double roundoff remains.

If overlap, choose `yTerminal=max(reach.yMinus,L)`, the intersection's closed
lower endpoint. Its upper endpoint is `min(reach.yPlus,U)`. Set
`betaTerminal=reach.yBarT-yTerminal`. Part VII proves an admissible progressive
lateral history exists for that point; no explicit ay reconstruction is needed.

Form the actual zT from yTerminal, reach.wT and the frozen longitudinal terminal
coordinates. Call P0.2.7 again and require dualCapable, finiteGeometryAvailable
and exact equality of YO/YTin/YTout/Yfwd to the probe slice. Then call frozen
global membership and require preservation.inSet. Either discrepancy gives
UNRESOLVED with its explicit cross-check reason; the actual states/results
remain in trace. Discrepancies are neither hidden nor repaired.

The successful chain is: applicable Lift initial gate, actual longitudinal
whole-prefix safety, exact progressive lateral reachability, finite terminal
overlap, and actual terminal dual capability/global membership. P5b-Lift and
P5b-T3 promote that endpoint certificate to whole-prefix shared preservation.
No reserve history or shared trajectory is numerically sampled to claim it.

Three margins describe fixed-T endpoint feasibility only. Zero margin is
contact, not horizon loss or a terminal obstruction classification.

## Output and failure semantics

```text
decision
  status,feasibleCertified,infeasibleCertified,reason
  initial: membership,capability,diagnostics,localCompatibility
  domain: p5bLift
  longitudinalSearch
  rhoSearch: rhoLower,rhoUpper,rhoValues,numPlannedCandidates,numCandidates,candidates
  feasibilityCertificate,infeasibilityCertificate
  numerical: exceptionIdentifier,exceptionMessage
```

`numCandidates` is the number actually examined and equals trace length;
`numPlannedCandidates` is the deduplicated full-grid size. A successful early
exit may inspect fewer than planned. Empty paths have zero candidates.

Each candidate records rho, reachability, geometryAvailable, YO/YTin/YTout/
Yfwd, all three margins, minMargin, phiDual, overlap, intersection endpoints
and reason. It also retains probeState/probeCapability and any attempted
actual terminalState/terminalCapability/terminalMembership for cross-check audit.

A positive-T feasibility certificate contains T, longitudinalWitness and
longitudinalCertificate, rho/reachability, all four frontiers, three margins
and phiDual, yTerminal/betaTerminal, actual terminal state/capability/membership,
and five true theorem flags: p5bLiftApplicable, longitudinalWholePrefixSafe,
progressiveLateralReachable, terminalDualCapable and intervalOverlap.
Its type is P5B_T3_FINITE_CHART. A T=0 certificate instead has type
ZERO_DURATION_GLOBAL_MEMBERSHIP, T, terminalState/terminalMembership and its
zero-duration/global-membership theorem flags; it fabricates no search or Lift.

Negative certificate types are only INITIAL_GLOBAL_NONMEMBERSHIP (membership0)
and INITIAL_LOCAL_INCOMPATIBILITY (cap0, diagnostics0, localCompatibility0).
Outside-domain and unresolved results have neither certificate type.

Exhausting the finite rho grid gives NO_CERTIFIED_JOINT_CANDIDATE. This does
not exclude another rho or another longitudinal witness/moment pair. Candidate
misses, missing terminal geometry and longitudinal failure never produce an
infeasibility certificate.

## Input errors and numerical limits

Malformed consumed Scenario/state data, T<0 or malformed own options raise
`AVLC:InvalidFixedHorizonFeasibilityInput`. Frozen-module Invalid errors are
translated to this interface ID without changing the frozen module. Validation
scope follows consumption: zero/initial-nonmember paths need only the global
membership inputs; positive capability paths also consume a valid full Scenario
as required by P0.2.7. Negative states are mathematical inputs, not malformed.

Frozen global/lateral arithmetic failures, nonfinite derived rho/margins or
inconsistent reachability return UNRESOLVED with a stage/reason and available
diagnostics. Arithmetic uncertainty cannot establish an exact negative. No
tolerance changes safety, overlap, domain signs or cross-check equalities.
